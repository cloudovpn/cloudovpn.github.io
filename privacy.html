1. Information Collection and Use

CloudVPN may process the following categories of information, depending on the features you use and the services enabled in the App:

  • Account and authentication information: If VPN access requires an account, your username, authentication credentials, access status and account expiration information may be processed to verify your access to VPN servers and prevent unauthorized use.
  • Connection and technical information: VPN servers necessarily process technical details such as your source IP address, the VPN server selected, and routing information needed to establish and operate a connection. Connection timing, connection errors and network diagnostics may also be processed or recorded for service operation, security and troubleshooting, depending on server configuration.
  • App settings: Preferences such as your selected server and connection options may be stored locally on your device to support App functionality.
  • Advertising information: If advertising is enabled, advertising partners may automatically process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and technical diagnostics to serve and measure advertisements and prevent abuse.
  • Usage analytics: If enabled, analytics services may process app events, installation or device identifiers, app version, device information and approximate region to help us understand App usage and improve performance.
  • Crash and diagnostic information: If enabled, crash-reporting services may collect crash reports, stack traces, device and operating-system information, app state and installation identifiers to identify and fix technical problems.
  • Information you send to us: If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message so we can respond to your inquiry.

Some information is processed on your device, while other information must be transmitted to VPN servers or third-party service providers to deliver the features described above. Therefore, it would not be accurate to state that all information stays on your device or that no information is collected.

2. VPN Service and Network Traffic

CloudVPN uses the Android VpnService API for its core VPN functionality. When you choose to connect and grant Android’s VPN permission, the App establishes an encrypted VPN tunnel between your device and a VPN server. Network traffic from apps covered by the VPN connection is routed through that tunnel.

VPN servers must process network packets and associated technical information to route traffic and provide connectivity. This may include source and destination addressing information and DNS-related routing data, where applicable. Encryption protects traffic between your device and the VPN tunnel endpoint; it does not mean that all activity on the wider internet is anonymous or end-to-end encrypted.

CloudVPN does not use the VPN tunnel to insert advertisements into other apps’ network traffic or to redirect third-party app advertising traffic for monetization. Ads, if shown, are displayed within CloudVPN using the advertising service described below.

The VPN connection operates only when you enable it and grant the required system permission. You can disconnect the VPN from within the App or through Android settings.

5. How Information May Be Shared

Information may be shared or processed by providers only as relevant to the App’s operation, including:

  • VPN hosting and infrastructure providers, to route your connection and maintain the service;
  • Google and its applicable service partners, when Google Play Services, AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase or Firebase Crashlytics are enabled; and
  • Authorities or other parties where legally required, or where reasonably necessary to protect rights, security and prevent misuse, in accordance with applicable law.

We do not use VPN browsing traffic as a product to sell to advertisers, and we do not manipulate traffic from other apps for advertising monetization.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information handled by CloudVPN. The VPN connection uses encryption between the Android device and the VPN endpoint. Other transmissions and storage practices depend on the specific services and infrastructure involved. No method of transmission, storage or security protection can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Please do not send passwords or other sensitive login secrets in support emails.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Account and access-related information is kept for the period needed to operate and administer the applicable VPN account. Technical connection records, where retained by server systems, are kept only for legitimate operational, security, troubleshooting or legal purposes. Analytics, advertising and crash-reporting information may be retained by the relevant third-party providers according to their own policies and settings.

When information is no longer needed for those purposes, we take steps to delete it or render it non-identifiable, subject to applicable legal obligations and necessary security or backup processes.

Request data or account deletion: If you have a CloudVPN account or believe we hold information related to your use of the App, email vpncyril@gmail.com with the subject “CloudVPN Privacy or Deletion Request”. Describe the account or information concerned without sending your password. We may request reasonable verification to protect the account. We will review the request and delete applicable account-related information in accordance with our obligations, except information that must or may lawfully be retained for legitimate reasons.

Removing the App clears its locally stored app data according to Android’s normal behavior, but uninstalling it may not automatically delete information already processed on a VPN server or by third-party providers.
