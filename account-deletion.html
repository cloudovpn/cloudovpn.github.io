Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of a CloudVPN account and related information, where applicable.

How to request deletion

Send an email to vpncyril@gmail.com with the subject CloudVPN Account Deletion Request.

Include the CloudVPN account username (if you have one) and a short description of your request. Never include your password in the email. We may ask you to verify that you control the account before acting on your request.

Request deletion →

What your request covers

For accounts administered by CloudVPN, we will review your request to delete the account and associated information in our control when applicable. Certain technical or security records may need to be retained where permitted or required by law. Information already processed by independent third-party providers is subject to their own retention and privacy practices.

If you only use public servers without a CloudVPN account, email us to ask about any related information that may be held.

More information

See our Privacy Policy for categories of information that may be processed, their purposes, retention considerations, and how to exercise privacy rights.
