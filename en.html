GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Note: VPN performance and server availability depend on your network and service conditions.

How does CloudVPN work?

CloudVPN uses Android’s VPN service to connect your device to a VPN server and route supported traffic through an encrypted tunnel.

Will my VPN speed always be the same?

Performance depends on your internet service provider, network conditions, server location, and server availability.

Why does Android ask for VPN permission?

Android displays a system permission prompt before allowing an app to establish a VPN connection. Review the prompt before approving.

Does using a VPN guarantee complete anonymity?

No. VPNs can help protect traffic between your device and the VPN server, but cannot guarantee complete anonymity or protection from every online threat.
